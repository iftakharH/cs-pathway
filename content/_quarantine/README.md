## 2026-11-05 — Stack Overflow pulse survey claim

The build brief supplied a claim that an April 2026 Stack Overflow pulse survey found agent
use had roughly doubled from 31% to 59%. The linked post was not read and the figure was not
confirmed against a primary or two independent secondary sources. It is not published.

To restore it: read the linked post in full, record the exact wording and method, add a
source record with a retrieval date, and add the claim with the right kind and confidence.
